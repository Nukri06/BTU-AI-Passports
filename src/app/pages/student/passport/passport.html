ABOUT
THE AI SKILLS

DIGITAL PASSPORT

The BTU AI Skills Digital Passport is your personalized and evolving record of competencies developed through your university and professional experience.

AI Skill Stamps may be earned through eligible academic courses, workshops, masterclasses, hackathons, intensive programs, guest speaker sessions, and other AI-related learning experiences.

Participation in the Passport is voluntary. There is no required number or combination of stamps and no predefined pathway. Students select learning opportunities according to their interests and skills.

As new competencies are developed and recognized, additional stamps are added to the Passport, progressively creating an individual competency profile.

UNDERSTANDING AI SKILLS STAMPS

AI SKILL

FOCUSED LEARNING

Awarded through an eligible AI-focused learning experience, such as a workshop, masterclass, seminar, or guest speaker session.

Represents: Development of a defined AI competency.

AI APPLIED SKILL

APPLIED LEARNING

Awarded through an eligible extended practical learning experience, typically involving a challenge, project, hackathon, or internship.

Represents: Hands-on application of a defined AI competency.

AI ADVANCED SKILL

EXTENDED ACADEMIC LEARNING

Awarded through the successful completion of an eligible semester-long academic course in which AI competency is a defined assessed component.

Represents: Extended and formally assessed development of an AI competency.
